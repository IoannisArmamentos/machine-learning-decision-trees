Assignment 1 - Question 3: CART (scikit-learn DecisionTreeClassifier)
=====================================================================

Dataset: Raisin_Dataset.xlsx, 900 examples, 7 numeric features, classes: Besni=450, Kecimen=450

Hold-out evaluation (test accuracy, leaves, depth):
  Stratified split, random_state=1, 630 training / 270 test examples.
  The same split is used for all four configurations; each tree is
  trained on the training set only.

Cross-validation (CV mean, CV std):
  StratifiedKFold(n_splits=10, shuffle=True, random_state=1) on the complete dataset.
  CV std is the population standard deviation (ddof=0) over the folds.

Classifier settings:
  random_state=1; all growth limits at their defaults (max_depth=None, etc.).
